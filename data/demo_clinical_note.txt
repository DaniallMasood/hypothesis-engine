CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Persistent fatigue, post-exertional malaise, racing heart on standing, and brain fog
for 18 months following COVID-19 infection in September 2023.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:
Patient is a 29-year-old female who contracted COVID-19 in September 2023, confirmed
by PCR. Initial illness was moderate — fever, cough, fatigue for 10 days. She never
fully recovered. At approximately 6 weeks post-infection she developed orthostatic
tachycardia (HR rising from 68 bpm supine to 122 bpm standing), severe fatigue,
cognitive difficulties described as "thinking through fog," and episodes of flushing
with urticaria.

She reports inability to exercise without 24-48 hour relapse of symptoms. A 2-day
CPET performed in January 2025 showed significant drop in VO2 max from Day 1 to
Day 2 (18.2 to 13.7 mL/kg/min), confirming post-exertional malaise.

Tilt table test positive for POTS criteria: HR increase >30 bpm within 10 minutes
of upright tilt without hypotension.

DIAGNOSES:
1. Long COVID (Post-acute sequelae of SARS-CoV-2)
2. Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS)
3. Suspected Mast Cell Activation Syndrome (MCAS) — pending tryptase confirmation
4. Cognitive impairment / brain fog
